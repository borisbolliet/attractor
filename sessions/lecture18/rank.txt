Lecture 18, a sketch of a matrix of rank 2: two random combinations of its columns span the same plane as its two eigenvectors.
    python -m doctest -v sessions/lecture18/rank.txt

>>> import numpy as np
>>> rng = np.random.default_rng(2)
>>> u, _ = np.linalg.qr(rng.standard_normal((5, 2)))
>>> A = 3.0 * np.outer(u[:, 0], u[:, 0]) + np.outer(u[:, 1], u[:, 1])
>>> np.linalg.matrix_rank(A)
np.int64(2)
>>> Y = A @ rng.standard_normal((5, 2))         # a sketch with two columns
>>> np.linalg.matrix_rank(np.column_stack([Y, u]))
np.int64(2)
